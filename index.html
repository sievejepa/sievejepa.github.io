SIEVE

A picture of a scene goes through an encoder and becomes a long list of numbers. SIEVE finds where in that list each thing in the scene is stored: the cube, its colour, the light, the camera.

The method at work on a made-up world.

How it works, in three lines

  • Take two pictures that differ in only a few things, and see which part of the list moves.
  • Let one more thing differ each time. The new part that moves belongs to the thing just added.
  • Take that piece out, and go on to the next.

What we found

In a clean made-up world, every factor was recovered almost perfectly. On robot pictures with an autoencoder, factors the encoder stores strongly were recovered strongly, and factors it barely stores were not. So the limit is the encoder, not the method.
